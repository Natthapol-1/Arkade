## Normal aliens and rewards

HP below is the configuration base, before [difficulty scaling](GAMEPLAY.md#difficulty-progression). Movement is px/tick. Standard direct damage is shown player / placed Ruby; special mechanics can use their own damage values.

| Alien / internal type | Base HP | Speed | Damage | Behavior and additional defeat reward |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Normal / `normal` | 5 | 0.68 | 10 / 6 | Chases player or Ruby; basic melee threat |
| Armored / `armored` | 22 | 0.47 | 15 / 10 | Toughest regular base HP; no inherent damage reduction. Grants player absorb shield of at least 10 |
| Runner / `fast` | 4 | 5.5 | 6 / 3 | Very fast and fragile; +8 additional energy |
| Bomber / `bomber` | 6 | 0.53 | Explosion | Winds up close to its target; explodes on ordinary defeat too. Explosion can damage player, placed Ruby and other aliens |
| Sniper / `sniper` | 5 | 0.34 | 13 / 13 | 10-tile ranged shot, 50-tick warning. Grants 5 seconds of chain lightning; chain range 4 tiles, damage 8 |
| Healer / `healer` | 11 | 0.42 | None | Restores nearby allies 2 HP each second within 3 tiles. Grants +24 player HP in addition to the usual +5 |
| Charger / `charger` | 17 | 0.75 idle | 20 / 14 | Locks a cardinal lane, warns 30 ticks, rushes at 7.5px/tick for up to 8 tiles. Player impact pushes one tile along the rush direction, including shielded hits. Push respects walls, finishes an in-flight step first and preserves held input; no push on Lunar Echo/Ruby or after a fatal hit. Wall stun 55 ticks, shorter hit/miss recovery; player wave push interrupts the Charger. Grants 2-second speed burst |
| Ghost / `ghost` | 4 | 0.89 | 12 / 9 | Passes through walls. Grants +15 HP to a placed Ruby |

| Mini Splitter / `mini_splitter` | 4 | 0.95 | 6 / 4 | Small attacker produced by a Splitter |
| Shielder / `shielder` | 10 | 0.44 | None | Follows/protects an ally within 8 tiles; halves damage exactly, including fractions. Grants Ruby absorb shield of at least 10 |
| Energy Leech / `energy_leech` | 8 | 1.8 | Energy theft only | Always hunts player. At one-tile range steals up to 20 available energy about every 75 ticks; stores the actual amount. Defeat returns it, capped at gauge maximum, plus ordinary rewards |
| Queen Echo / `queen_echo` | 1 | Stationary | 5 / 4 | Permanent shooting copy left by Queen; defeat it or kill Queen to clear it. Not a natural spawn-pool alien |

Most ordinary attackers choose player versus placed Ruby by proximity; carrying directs them toward the player. Healer/Shielder retain support AI; Leech explicitly ignores Ruby as a target. Charger damage occurs on actual tile arrival, not before contact. Its committed lane can be dodged sideways.

Leech deals no HP damage, does not drain shields or active powered time, and never creates energy from an empty gauge. Lunar Echo takes a decoy hit instead of losing real energy. God mode prevents theft. Meteor defeat returns only its stored energy, without standard kill rewards. Devourer absorption and debug removal are not defeats and give no refund. Storage is `Enemy.stolenEnergy`; display cells saturate at 60, but stored energy itself can exceed that.

Absorb shields take damage before HP; ordinary reward shields refill a minimum pool, rather than stacking. Shielder's 50% reduction is separate from these pools and is applied before a Frost shield. Keep fractional damage; do not round low damage up to 1.

## Boss encounters

Ten mutually exclusive boss encounters. Astral Twins uses two linked bodies; the pair counts as one encounter. First natural boss appears around 60 seconds. After defeat, next boss arrives after about 42 seconds (38.5s countdown + 3.5s warning). Timer freezes while a boss is alive. The natural random pool covers all ten encounters before refilling and avoids immediately repeating the previous boss at a pool boundary. Explicit God spawns do not consume that pool.

Spawn HP = `base HP + 15 * prior bosses defeated`; Twins split that stage bonus equally between their two bodies. Boss defeat restores player and Ruby HP fully, fills star energy and grants the appropriate celestial skill starting at the tenth defeat. Boss defeat rewards also apply to meteor kills. Removal must clean up that boss's curse/hazards; Queen death clears her echoes.

| Shadow Queen / `splitter_queen` | 50 | Stationary; shoots through cover in her chamber within 16 tiles, with 40-tick windup. Every 8s jumps to a different chamber excluding the player's, leaves a permanent Echo and heals 7% max HP capped at 8. Direct damage 7 player / 10 Ruby |
| Storm Reaper / `storm_reaper` | 60 | Hunts player only at speed 4. Close slash for 20 damage, 24-tick cooldown. When both are in the same hallway, seals its end caps for 4s, 8s seal cooldown; floor remains traversable inside. Death immediately lifts seal |
| Soul Devourer / `devourer` | 35 | Swarm boss: the fight is chasing down its copies, not one big body. Speed 3.4 (stacks never slow it). At 0–1 stacks it paths to the nearest regular alien anywhere to eat first (still biting anything in reach); at 2+ stacks, or with no aliens left, it hunts the nearer of player/placed Ruby. Bite: 2-tile reach (never grows), 5 player / 1 Ruby damage, 60-tick cooldown, +0.5 damage per stack. The original gains only 6 HP per prior boss defeat (other bosses 15) and is born with 1 stack (copies start at 0). Eats the nearest eligible regular alien within 5 tiles. Each meal below the 3-stack cap adds a stack and +5% max HP (healed); at the 3-stack cap a meal heals only 1%. The eating cooldown starts after each meal: 6s for the original, then each copy generation adds 1s more than the last (+3s, +4s, +5s: 9s, 13s, 18s, 24s, ...); the original can eat immediately. On death, each stack bursts out as a copy on nearby floor with 50% of the dead body's max HP and no stacks; copies are 10% smaller, 10% slower (speed × 0.9) and bite for 20% less (damage × 0.8) per generation. Newborn copies flee the player at 1.5× speed without biting for 2s (violet/green speed streaks) and can eat after 3s; they can eat and split again. Shared safety cap: the whole family never has more than 10 living bodies; a death spawns only as many copies as fit under it. Ignored bodies self-split: after 20s alive, a body with at least one stack spends one to bud a newborn copy (same copy rules, within the cap), then its 20s timer restarts; a pulsing pale-green frame warns during the last 2s. The encounter, boss timer freeze, reward and celestial skill end only with the last living Devourer body; earlier bodies give no rewards except an 8 HP player heal each (plus the kill-healing Constellation bonus; not for meteor kills). |

| Blind Prayer / `blind_prayer` | 80 | Globally reverses movement while alive. Flees at speed 2 when the player is within 24 tiles (same escape range as Thorn Weaver); otherwise wanders. | Sea Chanter / `sea_chanter` | 90 | Hunts at speed 3.8 with warned traveling water attacks and 6-tile splash (15 center / 5 edge); traveling water hits deal 10 damage. While diving takes 1.6× HP damage, including meteor, and heals from player damage to regular aliens anywhere: 80% actual HP lost, max 25 per hit/alien. | Thorn Weaver / `thorn_weaver` | 80 | Flees at speed 2.8 and aims at player anywhere on map. | Aegis Sentinel / `aegis_sentinel` | 90 | Hunter: fast while shielded (speed 4.0, slightly above Sea Chanter), slow at 1.19 with its core exposed; nearest player/placed Ruby targeting; 12/8 damage within 2 tiles, 75-tick cooldown. Starts shielded with two stationary constructs in two distinct random chambers. Shield reduces every damage source to 5% and reflects 100% of player-dealt damage (before that reduction) to the player on every hit, with no cap or cooldown: bullets, lasers, waves, bombs, chain lightning and celestial damage. Meteors, Supernova and enemy damage are reduced but not reflected. Player shields, guard and protection modes apply normally. Each construct has 24 + 3 per prior defeat HP; meteors deal 50% of construct max HP per strike. All normal player attacks can destroy them. They cannot be pushed, receive support healing/shields or be consumed, and give no kill rewards. After 20s alive, each pylon charges for 3s. Damage does not reset its lifetime or cancel its charge; only destroying it prevents the overload. On overload, it deals 80 damage only to the player in its chamber (normal protection applies), then restores its HP and reforms in another random chamber excluding its previous chamber and the other pylon’s chamber. Menus and Eclipse pause the overload timer; Ruby, enemies and the boss are unaffected by the blast. Both destroyed exposes the core immediately for 20s of gameplay; the last 3s show a closing-frame warning with a chime each second, then a fresh pair restores the shield. Defeat/removal clears surviving constructs |
| Astral Twins / `astral_twins` | 60 each | Spawn 6–8 tiles apart in one chamber, each gaining 7.5 HP per prior defeat. Both target the player. Dusk moves at speed 3.8 and is ranged: 9-tile reach, 8 damage, a 36-tick (0.6s) tracking charge and 100-tick cooldown after firing. Like Queen, its shot targets the player’s current position and ignores cover, without a hit/miss roll. Leaving the chamber or range cancels the charge; normal protection and Lunar Echo apply. Finishing both within that window gives one stage, one score reward and one full restore. Every 6–9s while both live, either eligible twin is randomly selected to donate 30% of its current HP to the other. The donor can have less HP than its partner; cost/gain is capped by recipient missing HP and donor HP above 1, preserving total HP. No transfers while either twin is down |

Encounter timers pause in menus. Eclipse stops these bosses’ movement/attacks and twin HP transfers; core exposure and revival timers continue during active gameplay. The gold/cyan Sentinel opens its armor when exposed; seal pylons crack with damage and glow amber during overload. Coral Dawn and blue Dusk have distinct melee/ranged poses, shared half-star emblems, and visible fall/revival effects. HP bars remain readable at game scale; a fallen twin's empty HP bar becomes its revival timer, a dim fill that drains to nothing; there are no overhead names, shield counts or pylon warning labels. God-panel summons initialize the complete encounter; No Mobs preserves constructs and both twins, while No Boss/replacement removes the whole encounter without rewards. After Ascension, Sentinel grants Venus Veil; Twins grant Binary Star.

Lunar Echo attracts all direct enemy targeting across the map, including bosses (except the Moon challenge Queen; see [Moon](MOON.md)). Attraction does not give unlimited attack range, chamber access or line of sight. Support/stationary bosses retain their behavior. Area hazards, real meteors and Prayer's Ruby ritual remain dangerous. AI temporarily substitutes the decoy target, then restores real player state in `finally`; never permanently move or hide the actual player/Ruby to implement decoy attraction.
