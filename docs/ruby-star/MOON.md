# Moon challenge

[Overview](README.md) · [Gameplay](GAMEPLAY.md#modes-and-lobby)

`/ruby-star/challenge/moon`: an illusion fight against the Shadow Queen. Neptune is about finding things and Gaia about holding ground; the Moon is about reading which Queen is real. Everything shared by challenges (stage 0, guardian-only boss, recharging celestial skill, win on defeat) is in [Gameplay](GAMEPLAY.md). Values live in `constants.ts` as `MOON_*`. Logic is `moon.ts` (`state.moon`, with short hooks in `engine.ts`), art is `moon-art.ts` (the theme switches on via `isMoonMap`), and the rule sheet is `MoonGuide.tsx`.

**Three phases:** phase 2 adds mirrors and mimics, phase 3 adds darkness. Defeating phase 3 wins.

## The fight

- **HP:** three phases of 2000 (6000 total) on a three-segment guardian bar (phase 3 left), with the name line SHADOW QUEEN · PHASE n. The bar is not live while she is disguised: it shows her HP as of her last reveal or jump (`shownHp`) and catches up while she is revealed and when she jumps, so a hit on a disguised target never shows at once. Her phase follows that shown HP and never goes back; each new phase shows a PHASE n banner. God panel: Phase 1/2/3 buttons jump to that phase at full phase HP, summoning her first if needed, and Mark Ready recharges the Moon Mark at once. Her own hits deal 0.4× of the usual Queen damage (about 2.8 to you, 4 to Ruby). Her jumps never heal her.
- **Arrival:** she first appears with her teleport sound and the same arrival frame as her jumps.
- **Disguise:** she wears her Echo's look (model, flicker, bolt colour and minimap dot), so she looks exactly like her illusions.
- **Illusions** (`moon_illusion`, 4 HP):
  - Her first jump brings 4 and every jump after brings 2, appearing the same instant she lands: one in her new chamber and the rest in chambers other than yours.
  - At most 6 are alive in phase 1 and 8 from phase 2 (`MOON_CLONE_CAPS`); past that the oldest fades out.
  - They are stationary and run her exact AI (target choice, range, 40-tick windup, 70-tick cooldown), shooting for 3 to you and 2 to Ruby.
  - They give no score, energy or kill rewards. Her jump no longer leaves permanent Echoes.
- **Reveal:** for the last 1.5s (90 ticks) before each jump, she shows her true model with a breaking-mask burst and sound (see [Audio](AUDIO.md#moon-reveal)), and takes 2.5× damage.
- **Shatter:** an illusion destroyed by anything other than a meteor bursts into a ring of 8 crystal shards. They fly about 9 tiles at 5px/tick and stop at walls. A shard deals 6 to you or 4 to a placed Ruby, once.
- **Delayed heal:** 30% of the HP she loses to your attacks is banked (`healBank`) and heals you by that amount when she next jumps, and Ruby by the same amount if it is with you (carried, or placed in your room). It never heals on the hit, so damage alone does not give her away right away. Illusions, meteors and other sources earn nothing.
- **Lunar Echo:** her illusions are drawn to your decoy like any enemy, but the real Queen ignores it and keeps shooting you, so a decoy can give her away. In code, the decoy-substituted enemy pass skips her and a second pass updates her against the real player.
- **Tell:** only the real Queen casts a reflection on the crystal floor: a faint violet glow and a mirrored tiara below her.
- **Moon Mark (C):** marks the nearest Queen or illusion for 6s (blinking in the last second), then recharges 10s from the cast. A HUD icon right of the celestial skill icon shows it, in the same style: ice-blue sigil glowing with a C hint when ready, dimmed with a filling perimeter while recharging. The mark rides on whoever wears it: an ice-blue sigil on the battlefield and an ice-blue frame on the minimap. If it was her, it follows her to her next chamber; an illusion's mark stays put when she jumps. It reveals nothing by itself.
- **Mirror mites** (`mirror_mite`, 1 HP, speed 1.1, melee 4 to you / 3 to Ruby) are about 53% of regular alien spawns (60% of what is left after the shade roll). Any hit kills one, and a kill heals you +10 on top of the usual +5. A kill also lights two rooms violet on the minimap for 5s, fading in the last second, or until she jumps: the room she is in and one other, in random order. They are the fight's healing source, offsetting her already-high base damage.
- **Shades** (`moon_shade`, 6 HP, speed 1.0, melee 6 to Ruby / 4 to you) are Ruby hunters: they always go for a placed Ruby wherever it is, and only come after you while you carry it (or at a Lunar Echo decoy). Spawns roll for a shade first: 12% of regular spawns are shades.
- **Mimics (phase 2+)** (`moon_mimic`, 5 HP, speed 0.9, harmless): from phase 2 one arrives every 6s on its own timer (`MOON_MIMIC_GAP`, at most 4 alive) on a free wall-side spot at least 5 tiles from you. Mimics never replace other spawns or count toward the regular alien cap. It never chases or attacks. In its own form it wanders its room for 2–4s; then it walks to the nearest free, reachable wall-side spawn spot (where she and her illusions stand), picking the next nearest if that one gets taken, and only disguises once it stands there. Once disguised it stays that way until destroyed, drawn exactly like an illusion (model, flicker, minimap dot) and counted by the Moon Mark. Disguised, it has only 1 HP (one laser or three bullets), stands still, cannot be pushed and never attacks, which is the tell: an illusion that never shoots. Killed, it gives normal alien rewards and no shards. State is `Enemy.mimic`; `updateMoonMimic` skips the normal AI while disguised.
- **Mirrors (phase 2+):** when phase 2 starts, every room gets 3 straight 3-tile strips of mirror floor (`state.moon.mirrors`) on open floor away from pads, kept apart from each other and never under you. Every 20s (`MOON_MIRROR_SHIFT`) they all move to new spots, the old panes fading out as the new ones fade in with a pale flash over 0.5s. They stay through phase 3. You can walk over them, but your laser (which fires in all four directions) and bullets stop at a mirror and bounce their full damage back into you (2.1 laser / 0.35 bullet, more when powered); nothing behind the mirror is hit. Waves, bombs and skills pass. The minimap shows them as faint silver marks.
- **Darkness (phase 3):** one random room is completely dark (`state.moon.dark`), moving to a different random room every 10s (`MOON_DARK_SHIFT`) with a 0.5s cross-fade. Inside it, the floor is solid black: walls, cover blocks, the room outline and its teleport pad stay visible, as do you, Ruby, your bomb, the Moon Mark and every attack effect, but enemies, pickups, mirrors and floor detail are hidden, on the minimap too (where the room shows as a black wash). Anything hit inside shows itself for 0.75s (`MOON_SEEN_TICKS`). While she is revealed, the Queen still shows on the minimap even inside the dark.
- **Meteors** deal 10% of one phase (200) to her and wipe illusions in that room without shards.
- **No space events.** Her defeat clears every illusion and shard.

## Rule sheet

The map opens its own rule sheet on entry (also on RULES): eleven animated cards with one short line each. They cover Lunar Echo under CELESTIAL SKILL (PRESS F), the hidden Queen, the decoy tell, the reveal, the special skill (Moon Mark), mirror mites, shades, mimics, mirrors, darkness and shattering illusions.

## Look

A shadowy crystal cavern under the lunar surface: crystalline, magical and mysterious. There is no moon in the sky, since you are already on it.

- **Room colours:** amethyst, violet, orchid and ice blue (`MOON_CHAMBER_COLORS`), used on the battlefield borders, minimap and teleport picker. Ice blue `#a9e6ff` is the magic accent throughout.
- **Cover blocks:** every block is solid crystal (`drawMoonCrystalBlock`). Each tile is cut differently (a diagonal split either way, or an off-centre point) and lit from the top left, mostly in the room colour with some orchid tiles. Some tiles hold a deeper inner facet that pulses with an ice-blue spark. Thin pale facet edges, a bright rim only where a block meets open floor, and a pale glint that sweeps diagonally across all crystal at once.
- **Floor:** near-black polished crystal. Two soft diagonal sheens sweep across it (a broad violet one and a thin ice-blue one), faint grain lines brighten as the sheen passes, and each crystal block reflects a fading glow into the floor tile below it. Large dark mist patches drift slowly over the floor. A few tiles hold a small set gem that twinkles, and rare runes (a square frame, a turning diamond, four corner marks) fade in and out. Pads stay clean.
- **Mirror mite:** a small blocky crystal bug: a pale lilac rim, a dark mirror face with a lit facet and a glint sweeping across it, two pale eyes, a mirror shard on its back and three pairs of bent legs that scuttle.
- **Mimic:** in its own form, a soft lavender-grey shapeshifter cube whose four horizontal slices keep sliding out of line, one large eye with an ice-blue glint, and a faint flicker of the Queen's tiara above it. Disguised, it is drawn exactly as an illusion.
- **Mirror tile:** a see-through framed silver-lilac pane (the floor shows beneath, so it reads as walkable) with a bright glint sliding across it. A bounced shot shows a pale streak from the mirror back to you and a small cross flash on the pane.
- **Shade:** a translucent hooded wraith in deep violet that flickers between see-through and nearly solid, with a lit left edge, a dark face, two ice-blue eyes that flare (and claws that come out) as it strikes, and a ragged tail of drifting blocks.
- **Motes:** violet and ice-blue motes with short tails rise through the rooms on fixed world columns.
- **Backdrop:** the dark of a vast geode. Stepped crystal spires rise from below and hang from above in two parallax layers; the near ones have glowing tips and pulsing veins. Faint prism shafts sway, deep violet mist drifts, lone crystal shards turn slowly, crystal dust twinkles, and a shadow vignette closes in at the top and bottom.
