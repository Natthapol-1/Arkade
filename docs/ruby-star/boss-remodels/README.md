## Design notes at time of archiving

Boss model identities. Every boss keeps its element but reads as a cosmic body, matching Aegis/Twins, the space events and the celestial casts: crisp stepped blocks, stars and orbits, glow only on rims and cores, no smooth diagonal strokes, rotated squares or blurred halos.

Enemy beams (King, Queen/echo, Warden, Sniper, Seer and other ranged shots) use `drawEnemyLaser`, matching the player laser: a straight outer band with only a light 6px glow, a solid middle band and a bright core, with snapped fragment blocks on top. Do not draw diagonal beams as stepped pixel staircases or with large shadow halos; keep warning seals, ripples and attack glows to about 3–6px of blur. Boss models cap every glow at 4px (including the attacking pre-draw glow and heal aura), so rims, eyes and cores stay sharp. Exception: the Shadow Queen and her echoes keep their original thick violet glow (16px breathing body glow, glowing tiara/eyes and a breathing aura square).

- King (solar sovereign): red armor rim with a lit upper band, shaded lower/side edges and gold corner trims; dark ember inset with layered magma plates split by pulsing molten seams; a gold-trimmed two-eye visor and a ringed Solar-suit sun core with short gold rays; eight stepped corona tongues ring the body, five stepped gold flare spikes form the crown with a ruby gem, shoulder flare pillars and two lower flame marks rise taller when attacking, and snapped embers drift up. No teeth or heat haze. His shot is a plasma beam with flame-block fragments.
- Queen: purple square face/body, tiara/large forehead jewel, three eyes, restrained stepped shoulder tassels and raised smaller crown base. All gems are stepped pixel diamonds; four orbiting stars are joined by a medium glowing violet band (4px soft outer band, 1.5px bright inner line) as a spinning constellation crown, with bold white-cored corner stars. Echo is translucent/glitching and shares her identity.
- Reaper (battery robot): a hovering mint-teal battery cell with a luminous rim, dark cell wall, + terminal on top, − plate below and twin flickering hover jets; a robotic visor whose scanner eye sweeps side to side and locks to a full white slit while striking; four charge cells on the casing fill as the real melee cooldown recovers and shimmer when full; two blades, each one solid rectangle with a pale top edge and dark lower edge, stick out horizontally in a T pose (white pulse running along each at rest) and whirl twice around the body over the real windup and release with eased speed, faint solid trail copies and tip sparks, ending back in the T pose; blades are drawn as pixel beams so they stay crisp at every angle; stepped arcs crackle from the terminal to one shoulder joint at a time (both while striking), with sparks off the whirling blade tips. Her slash is twin stepped plasma pixel beams with an axis-aligned shock square and four stepped ion bolts.
- Devourer: violet square, large green eye, four-fang mouth, fused side chunks with swallowed eyes, three rectangular antennae on each side. Body grows 6% per stack; preserve feeding expression/aura/stack counter. Her bite sends stepped block crescents (pale centre, green body, violet tips) down a faint pixel tether.
- Warden (cryo core): the original frost-shield snowflake (a softly glowing stroked hexagon with six branched rays; an intentional exception to the blocky rule) backs a crisp pale ice-block casing (lit top edge, shaded base) around a dark navy panel with a frosted window grid and four ice corner studs; a pulsing eight-point snowflake core with a white centre is her eye and widens while she channels a shield; stepped three-block crystal spikes (12/10/6 units) sit at each corner and grow outward during the shield windup and release; four ice cubes ride a flat orbit (rear half behind the casing), a crisp square frost pulse marks each shield release, and small snowflakes drift down beside her.
- Graviton Seer (`blind_prayer`, tech gravity): a gold chassis (lit top edge, shaded base) around a dark gunmetal face plate; a long black wraparound gaming visor with gold trim and a bridge notch covers the eyes, wider than the head; gaming headphones fitted to the head (a slim gold-trimmed padded band with a sensor mast, gold-edged earcups over the sides of the head on top of the visor ends whose LED rings chase around and light fully while she invokes, and a short mic boom to the mouth panel); a gold LED scan sweeps across the visor, and three pale slit eyes burn through it while she invokes; a chest graviton core whose light is pulled inward through circuit traces; faint square gravity fronts contract toward the core (faster while invoking), two graviton beads orbit on a flat ellipse, gold motes spiral inward and twin hover thrusters flicker below. No cloth, glyphs, halo or arms.
- Chanter: blue square siren model, pale eyes and blocky water details. Underwater view is faint/narrow back-and-fin silhouette with bubbles, no face; health bar dims with her. Her splash footprint is a crisp tinted cosmic tide (no per-cell glow) with seven twinkling star glints.
- Weaver (star seed): square olive/green bark body, a stepped crystal-leaf crown with pale facets and glinting tips, an off-center pink four-point star bloom on a bent stalk, vines/root feet and rising spore stars. **Five eyes total: one main eye and one small eye in each cardinal direction.** Thorn fields hold a sparse scatter of stepped crystal thorns with twinkling tips.
