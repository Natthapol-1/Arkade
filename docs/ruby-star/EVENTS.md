# Space events

[Overview](README.md)

Meteors remain the regular main hazard. Four minor events occur occasionally and never overlap each other. Every event gives a three-second warning, `INCOMING: EVENT NAME 3s` (live countdown, no chamber name) and minimap markers before activating. Warnings and events pause with rules, teleport selection and Constellation choices, and stop advancing after game over. Restart clears them.

Natural first warning is scheduled after 150–210 seconds of unpaused play. After an event finishes or its shard is collected, the next warning waits 180–300 seconds. Due events wait for active meteor warnings and boss warning/spawn announcements to clear. A shuffled 20-slot pool contains six copies each of Black Hole, Shooting Star and Eclipse, plus two Supernovas (`SUPERNOVA_POOL_COPIES`). Supernova therefore has the lowest natural share (2/20, 10%); repeats are possible. Meteors continue independently and may happen during an already-active minor event. Manual God triggers retain the warning, reject overlapping events and do not consume the natural pool.

| Event | Active duration | Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Black hole | 7 seconds | Fixed at tile (25,25), in the central void between chambers. Every 45 ticks, pulls player and all eligible aliens/bosses one cardinal tile toward the center, respecting walls. Uses normal forced-movement interpolation for the player, waits for existing steps/knockback and does not move a frozen player. Push interrupts Charger lanes. No direct damage and no movement of placed Ruby. Players can continue controlling movement between pulls. |
| Shooting star | 0.6-second fall, then about 30 seconds to collect | Warns a random interior floor tile outside the currently warned meteor chamber. Falls without dealing damage, leaving a golden stellar shard. Collect it for +20 player HP, +20 Ruby HP, full star energy and a free speed burst. Its next full-energy power activation lasts 4 seconds instead of 2, plus Phoenix duration if owned. This bonus waits through any already-active power, does not stack with another stellar shard, and is consumed with the gauge (including Sirius). HP/energy caps and burst-duration upgrades apply. Cannot collect before landing; expires unclaimed. Separate from ordinary resources and their five-item cap. |
| Supernova | Up to 45 seconds dormant; 3-second triggered charge; 2.5-second blast effect | Appears on a random safe floor tile. Player lasers, waves, bomb blasts and damaging celestial area effects (Solar/Jupiter, Singularity and Gaia pulses) trigger it; bullet, chain lightning, contact, meteor and enemy attacks do not. Once hit, shrinks and charges for 3 seconds, with no retrigger/extension. Untouched stars expire harmlessly. The explosion hits all living aliens once across cover and chambers, with quadratic distance falloff: regulars take `5 + 95 * max(0,1-distance/65)^2`, bosses take `maxHP * (0.05 + 0.55 * max(0,1-distance/65)^2)`, distance in tiles. Player damage is distance-based: 100 within 20 tiles, then `ceil(100 * ((45-distance)/25)^3)` between 20 and 45 tiles, zero at 45+. The diagonally opposite chamber is always immune. Normal player guard and shields apply. Ruby is immune; underwater vulnerability still applies to enemy damage. Environmental damage cannot feed Chanter or trigger player electricity; defeats use meteor-style rewards/cleanup, including boss restoration, skills and Constellations. |
| Eclipse | 6 seconds | Pauses hostile AI movement, attack windups, boss abilities, traveling Sea Chanter attacks and Thorn/poison hazard updates. Player movement, abilities, friendly fields, ordinary cooldowns and meteors continue. AI resumes when it ends. Reactive bomber death explosions and the player's own bomb remain dangerous. |

Event placement uses walkable interior floor, avoids pads and placed Ruby, and starts at least three tiles from the player. Black hole/Eclipse use the center as a visual/minimap anchor rather than a walkable spawn.

## Implementation

`events.ts` owns scheduling, warning/active state and gravity. `GameState.ambientEvents` stores timers, pool, active positions and shard reward feedback. `updateAmbientEvents` returns one-time shard-collection and Supernova-detonation signals; `engine.ts` applies existing heal/energy/speed reward helpers. Event updates run against the real player before Lunar AI target substitution. Eclipse gates hostile updates without pausing player-owned fields or meteors. `page.tsx` draws actual event state with crisp glowing rectangles and shows warnings/active feedback.

The God panel is at the upper left, opposite the minimap, and groups Immortal and Invincible in one two-column row, followed by Full HP and Full Energy together. Invincible ignores damage; Immortal allows damage but keeps player and Ruby at 1 HP minimum. The protection toggles are mutually exclusive and persist through restart. Full HP instantly restores player and Ruby HP to their caps without restarting the run; disabled after game over. Black Hole, Shooting Star, Eclipse and Supernova each have a dedicated button in a compact two-column grid. Event buttons are disabled while a minor event/warning or brief pickup-feedback interval is active, so event triggers do not interrupt pickup feedback. The debug panel draws above touch ability controls so its scrolled controls remain usable.

Meaningful checks: `tests/ambient-events.test.cjs` covers scheduling, pause, wall-safe pulls, rewards and Eclipse; `tests/supernova.test.cjs` covers rarity, laser/area triggers, charge timing, field-wide falloff, shields and boss rewards. `scripts/check-ruby-supernova.cjs` exercises real charged-wave input, announcements, pause, detonation and reset on desktop/mobile; `scripts/check-ruby-events.cjs` verifies all God triggers, rules pause, reset, shard collection and desktop/mobile rendering.
