# Ruby Star — Agent Notes

A top-down, chamber-based space-station defense game. Four chambers (ALPHA/BETA/GAMMA/DELTA) in a 2×2 grid, linked by hallways, each with a teleport pad at its center. Guard the Ruby Core — if either it or the player hits 0 HP, it's game over. 12 enemy types, 8 boss encounters (mutually exclusive), a meteorite hazard, and 5 player abilities.

## Files

- **`engine.ts`** — All core game logic and state. Contains the `GameState`/`Enemy` interfaces, the main `tick()` loop, per-enemy-type update dispatch (`updateEnemies`), all ability handlers (`useLaser`, `useBomb`, `activateWave`, `useSpeedBoost`, `useBullet`, `toggleCarryRuby`, `tryActivateTeleport`/`doTeleport`/`cancelTeleport`, `healRuby`), the 8-way boss spawn timer/`godSpawnBoss`, and the meteorite system. Operates independently of rendering — no DOM/canvas references.
- **`constants.ts`** — Map generator (`generateMap`), chamber bounds/spawn tiles, hallway bounds (`HALLWAY_BOUNDS`/`hallwayOfTile`), tile types, `EnemyType` union + `ENEMY_CONFIGS` (hp/speed/color/damage/cooldown per type), difficulty tiers, and every tuning constant for abilities and all eight bosses.
- **`page.tsx`** — The React component: `requestAnimationFrame` game loop, keyboard/mouse/touch input, the `drawGame`/`drawEnemy`/`drawMinimap` canvas rendering, `RulesModal` content, HUD, and the mobile-only virtual joystick + ability buttons (`TouchAbilityButton`).
- **`RubyStarGuide.tsx`** — Concise two-sheet guide inside `RulesModal`: main controls/rules/hints, plus an Aliens & Bosses sheet with 12 alien entries and 8 boss descriptions paired with celestial skills. Switching sheets keeps gameplay paused; reopening starts on the main guide. Styles are scoped in `page.tsx`.

## State shape (`engine.ts`)

- `GameState`: `gamePhase` (`'playing' | 'teleporting' | 'lost'`), player position/stats, ruby position/HP (`rubyTileX/Y === -1` means it's being carried), `enemies: Enemy[]`, per-ability cooldowns, `starEnergy`/`poweredTicks`, meteorite timers, boss timers (`bossTimer`, `bossWarningTicks`, `bossesKilled`), all visual-FX arrays (`laserBeams`, `waveEffects`, `bombBlasts`, `lightningArcs`, `deathParticles`), and teleport state (`teleportDestOptions`, `teleportCooldown`).
- `Enemy`: shared fields (`tileX/Y`, `targetTileX/Y`, `stepProgress` for tile-to-tile interpolation, `hp`, `attackTimer`, `windupTicks`, `shootTicks`, `flashTicks`, `healFlashTicks`). Several fields are dual-purpose per type — see gotchas below.

## Non-obvious behavior

- **Latest progression/balance fixes:** Shielder protection halves fractional damage without rounding or minimum-hit inflation. Opening difficulty increases at 10/25/45 seconds, then remains at tier 3 until boss progression; stages 2/3/4/5 unlock tiers 4/5/6/7. First boss remains around one minute. Natural bosses use a random pool without replacement, covering all eight per cycle and avoiding an immediate repeat across cycles; explicit God spawns do not consume it. Carrying speed is now 60% of normal, per the user's correction from the proposed 70%.
- **Status feedback:** compact tags appear in the playfield only while frozen/chilled, controls reversed or healing reduced; timed statuses include a countdown/progress line. Teleporting with Ruby still auto-places it, now with a two-second pink placement cue and square world pulse; pickup clears that cue. All timers pause with gameplay. Game-over heading is RUBY STAR and progression reads STAGE (bossesKilled + 1).

- **Healing works while carrying Ruby.** Space/mobile HEAL restores 5 Ruby HP and 3 player HP, with the existing cooldown and enemy-clear requirement. A carried core is always in healing range. Space still heals on teleport pads; X/mobile TP is a separate teleport control. Jupiter's player suit has reverted to its original bands and red storm spot; the tiny cloud curl/highlight trial was removed.

- **Teleportation pauses the whole game.** `tick()` returns immediately (`if (state.gamePhase === 'teleporting') return;`) — enemies, cooldown timers, the meteorite clock, and the boss spawn timer all freeze while the destination menu is open. Press X/mobile TP on a pad to open it; picking a chamber or pressing Escape/X resumes normal ticking. Space only heals, including on pads, and does not select/cancel a teleport destination. Mobile HEAL and TP are separate buttons.
- **Bomb hurts the player, never the Ruby.** `useBomb`'s detonation damages any enemy in blast radius, damages the player if they're still in range (35% of the bomb's damage), but has no `damageRuby` call at all — the Ruby Core is immune to your own bomb.
- **Power-up window, not a charge.** Reaching max `starEnergy` immediately consumes it and grants `poweredTicks = 120` (~2s). *Any* ability fired during that window is enhanced (more range/damage/duration) — it's a timed buff, not a single-use token.
- **Fiery King vs. Shadow Queen vs. Storm Reaper vs. Soul Devourer vs. Frost Warden vs. Blind Prayer are mutually exclusive.** The spawn timer freezes while a boss is alive and resets when it dies (`BOSS_SPAWN_INTERVAL`, 38.5s plus a 3.5s warning = 42s after death). It checks all eight types before allowing a new warning — only one boss-tier enemy exists at a time, and which type spawns is a random pick of the eight made at spawn time (the warning banner stays generic "BOSS APPROACHING" either way, so the type is a surprise). The very *first* boss of a run is deliberately delayed further than subsequent ones — `createInitialState` seeds `bossTimer` with `BOSS_FIRST_SPAWN_TIMER` instead of `BOSS_SPAWN_INTERVAL`, sized so the first spawn lands around 60s in (accounting for the `difficultyLevel >= 1` gate at ~10s and the warning banner's ~3.5s), while every boss after that still uses the shorter `BOSS_SPAWN_INTERVAL`. A god-mode-only debug tool (`godSpawnBoss`, gated behind `?god=1`) can force-spawn any of the eight on demand, despawning whichever boss currently exists (queen decoys are untouched; an active hallway seal or Frost Warden ice-tile set is also lifted immediately).
- **Frost Warden (`frost_warden`) is a stationary field-controller with zero direct offense.** She never chases and never attacks the player or ruby (`damageToPlayer`/`damageToRuby` are 0 and unused). Her threat is entirely passive: while the player shares her chamber (`isPlayerChilled`, checked fresh every tick, not stored), player move speed is multiplied by `FROST_CHILL_SPEED_MULT` (0.65) and the LASER/BULLET/WAVE cooldowns (and the WAVE hold-to-charge duration in `page.tsx`) tick down at `FROST_CHILL_TICK_RATE` (0.5x — effectively half attack speed) instead of the normal 1/tick. On spawn and every `FROST_RELOCATE_INTERVAL` (~30s, reuses `phaseTimer` like the Queen's phase-jump) she teleports to a different chamber and calls `scatterFrostIceTiles` to pick `FROST_ICE_TILE_COUNT` (7) truly random floor tiles from that chamber's interior (scanning `CHAMBER_BOUNDS` for `T_FLOOR`, not the perimeter-only `CHAMBER_SPAWN_TILES` pool that was used at first) as hazards (`state.frostIceTiles`, not a tile-type mutation — the underlying tile stays `T_FLOOR`, hazard membership is just a coordinate-list lookup). Stepping onto one of these tiles sets `state.playerFrozenTicks = FROST_FREEZE_DURATION` (~1.5s), which makes `updatePlayer` return immediately at the top — full immobilization, not just a slow — and plays a thaw sound the instant it expires. Her only "offense" is supportive: every `FROST_SHIELD_CAST_DELAY` (~5s from spawn and recurring, reuses `windupTicks`) she fires a visible ice-laser (`castFrostShield`) at one random non-boss ally sharing her chamber, granting `FROST_SHIELD_HP` (15) points of `icyShieldHP` — a real absorb pool (`damageEnemy` drains it before touching `hp`), not a %-reduction like the shielder's. Any number of allies can be carrying a shield at once, since it's a per-`Enemy` field, not a single global slot. Dying (or being god-mode-despawned) clears `frostIceTiles` immediately.
- **Soul Devourer (`devourer`) grows mid-fight by eating other enemies.** Targets whichever of player/ruby is closer, like the King, and attacks at range with a "sound wave" burst (`state.laserBeams`, color `'#6b2fa8'`, rendered with a dedicated wavy/rippling `isDevourerLaser` style in `page.tsx` rather than a solid bolt) instead of a plain melee bite — her attack range grows exponentially from 1 to 40 tiles (roughly two chamber widths) across 0–20 absorb stacks: 1/3/8/19/40 at 0/5/10/15/20 stacks, using normalized (16^progress - 1)/15, floored to whole tiles. Early growth is small, late growth steep; absorption radius remains 5 tiles. Every `DEVOURER_ABSORB_COOLDOWN` (~9s — deliberately slow so reaching all 20 stacks takes ~3 minutes minimum even with a constant supply of nearby grunts), she finds the nearest non-boss ally within `DEVOURER_ABSORB_RANGE` and consumes it outright (removed from `state.enemies`, no kill reward for the player). Below `DEVOURER_MAX_STACKS` (20) this *compounds* her `maxHp` by `DEVOURER_STACK_HP_PCT` (12%, and heals her by the same amount gained), scales her damage on an accelerating quadratic curve (`DEVOURER_STACK_DMG_MAX_MULT`, base 12 dmg → 90 at full stacks — small early jumps, big ones late) per stack, and slows her by `DEVOURER_STACK_SPEED_PCT` (4%, linear, floored at 0.15 speed) per stack. **She never stops eating once maxed out** — at 20 stacks, absorbing an ally no longer grows her further, it just tops her `hp` back to `maxHp` (a free heal-to-full), so parking nearby grunts near a fully-stacked Devourer to bait a heal doesn't actually make her stronger. The compounding HP growth is deliberate — at 20 stacks she's roughly 10x her base HP (576) and 2x her base damage (90), "insanely broken" if a fight is neglected long enough to let her feast unchecked, but harmless if the grunts near her are cleared or she's rushed early. Two fields are reused for her: `chargeDirX` (unused otherwise for this type) holds the absorb-stack count, and `windupTicks` (also unused otherwise) holds the absorb cooldown countdown.
- **Storm Reaper (`storm_reaper`) always hunts the player, never the ruby**, unlike the King and Queen (both of which target whichever of player/ruby is closer/present) — a deliberate "assassin" identity (`damageToRuby: 0`). She BFS-chases at a flat speed (no ramp, unlike the King) and melee-strikes at range 1. If she and the player are both standing in the same hallway (`hallwayOfTile`), and neither a seal is already active nor its cooldown (`REAPER_SEAL_COOLDOWN`) is up, she seals that hallway — `setHallwayWalled` walls off only the two end-caps of its `HALLWAY_BOUNDS` rect (not the whole corridor — sealing every tile stranded whoever was inside with zero adjacent floor tiles, freezing them in place instead of trapping them in a fightable space) — for `REAPER_SEAL_DURATION` (~4s), then reopens automatically. Since hallways are pure floor by construction (no cover-walls or teleport pads inside them, confirmed against `generateMap`), the wall swap is always clean and reversible. The seal state lives on `GameState` (`hallwaySeal`/`hallwaySealTicks`/`hallwaySealCooldown`), not per-enemy, since only one Reaper can ever exist. Dying (or being god-mode-despawned) immediately lifts an active seal rather than waiting out the timer.
- **Shadow Queen never chases.** She stays put in her chamber, firing through walls (no line-of-sight check, just same-chamber + `QUEEN_ATTACK_RANGE` distance) at whichever of player/ruby is present. Every `QUEEN_PHASE_INTERVAL` (~8s) she teleports to a different chamber — excluding both her current chamber *and* whichever chamber the player is currently in — leaving a `queen_echo` behind at her old tile.
- **Fiery King targets whichever of player/ruby is closer** (mirrors the Queen's split-attention behavior, computed fresh each tick via `tileEuclidDist` rather than gated by chamber like hers) — he'll BFS straight for an undefended ruby if it's nearer than the player. Only the Reaper is exempt from this and stays player-exclusive.
- **Echoes persist until killed.** `queen_echo` has 1 HP, keeps firing on the same windup/range rules as the Queen, and does *not* expire on a timer — `phaseTimer` is unused for this type (only meaningful for `splitter_queen`, where it's the phase-jump countdown). Ignoring the Queen's jumps lets echoes stack indefinitely. Killing an echo directly goes through `killEnemy` like any regular enemy — same starEnergy gain and passive HP heal (its `scoreValue` is 0, so no score). Killing the *real* Queen instead dissolves every remaining echo instantly via a direct `splice` in the boss-reward block, bypassing `killEnemy` entirely — that mass cleanup grants no reward per echo (`killEnemy`'s `skipRewards` flag now only covers meteor kills).
- **Meteor strikes cycle chambers clockwise, not randomly.** Order is ALPHA(TL) → BETA(TR) → GAMMA(BR) → DELTA(BL) → repeat, tracked by `meteoriteChamberSeq` indexing into `METEOR_CLOCKWISE_ORDER`. All five boss types take `BOSS_METEOR_DMG`% of their *current* max HP (not insta-killed like every other enemy type in the struck chamber) — for the Soul Devourer this uses her post-absorb, grown `maxHp`, so a heavily-stacked one still takes a meaningful hit. For the King/Queen/Reaper/Frost Warden, anything already at or below that HP fraction dies outright; the **Soul Devourer is the one exception** — a meteor alone can never kill her, her HP is floored at 3% of her current `maxHp` instead of being allowed to hit 0. `queen_echo` has no special case and dies outright like normal enemies. Note meteor strikes only target `CHAMBER_BOUNDS`, not hallways, so a Reaper lurking mid-hallway is untouched by them.
- **Ruby Carrying:** picking up the Ruby (E/F) halves player movement speed but makes enemies target *only* the player; placing it lets enemies split attention and attack the Ruby directly.
- **Death particles vs. FX arrays.** Every enemy death spawns a small colored particle burst (`spawnDeathParticles`, reuses the enemy's own config color) via `state.deathParticles` — separate from the beam/wave/blast FX arrays, and separate again from `spawnFireBurst` (used specifically for the Fiery King's laser landing on the player, orange/yellow embers with a slight upward kick).
- **King vs. Queen vs. Reaper vs. Soul Devourer vs. Frost Warden are visually unrelated on purpose.** The Fiery King's ambient effects (rising embers, ground-fire glow, flickering flame-lick spike tips, fire-gradient laser with an ember-storm trail), the Shadow Queen's (orbiting motes, revolving segmented ring, twinkling gems, magic-shimmer laser with an arcane-ripple + dust-storm trail), the Storm Reaper's (rotated-diamond core, angular blade fins, orbiting zigzag electric arcs, motion-streak afterimages, no crown/tiara since she's a rogue not royalty), the Soul Devourer's (violet/green corruption palette, body that physically grows and sprouts fused chunk-blocks with each absorb stack, single glowing eye, blocky maw that widens when attacking, always-on aura ring + orbiting spore particles), and the Frost Warden's (icy blue-white palette, crystalline 3-spike icicle crown instead of a gold crown or tiara, drifting frost-mote orbit, wavy rippling "sound wave"-style frost bolt) are each built from different primitives so none read as a reskin of the others. All five still use the same blocky, rectangle-based construction style as the rest of the game's sprites (`fillRect`/`strokeRect`, not smooth curves) — an earlier organic/curved take on the Soul Devourer (tendrils, circular chunks) was reworked into blocks for consistency, and the Frost Warden was built blocky from the start. The `EnemyType` key is `'fiery_king'`; some internal identifiers (`isBossLaser`, `godSpawnBoss`, etc.) still say "boss" as a naming holdover and aren't tied to the type string.
- **Mobile controls are DOM siblings, not descendants, of the canvas's touch-handled wrapper div.** The virtual joystick and ability-button cluster in `page.tsx` sit in separate fixed-position elements after the canvas wrapper closes, so a touch starting on them never bubbles into the wrapper's own `onTouchStart/Move/End` (swipe-to-move) or `onMouseDown/Up` handlers. They're shown/hidden purely via CSS (`@media (hover: none) and (pointer: coarse)`), keyed off actual input capability rather than viewport width, so anything with a real mouse never renders or sees them. Every button calls the *same* engine function keyboard/mouse call (e.g. the LASER button reuses `mouseDownTimeRef` so hold-to-charge-WAVE behaves identically) — there is no parallel mobile-only game logic.
- **Canvas Rendering:** `drawEnemy` has fully custom, early-`return`ing draw branches for `fiery_king`, `splitter_queen`, `queen_echo`, `storm_reaper`, `devourer`, and `frost_warden` (each builds its own body/eyes from scratch); every other type shares one generic blocky-body-plus-per-type-antenna/eyes path at the bottom of the function.

## Blind Prayer (`blind_prayer`)

Clean square yellow-and-black alien in the same simple style as Queen/Chanter. Approved design: 50×50 gold rim, flat charcoal and warm inner panels, three tiny forehead marks, matching translucent gold hooks at both upper and lower side corners (vertically mirrored), compact gold halo, tiny black horn tips, and 28 animated golden/black motes in clear side lanes. No hood, arms, feet, or mouth. Five translucent gold lower hems have uneven 10/17/7/13/9-unit lengths for a ghost-like silhouette; these replace the two detached blocks below the body. Single rectangular ancient cloth blindfold at 86% opacity; three blocky yellow X eye marks visible through it (55% gold), built from five 2×2 squares per eye. Three compact white ancient glyphs across the lower body. Gameplay effects and animation remain; only appearance changes. Starts at 80 HP and 2 px/tick movement. Its escape attack has 6-tile range (matching normal player wave), 6 damage, a 0.6s visible windup, and a 12s cooldown and a one-tile knockback away from the boss (stops at walls); it grants 2.5s at 5.4 px/tick even if the player dodges. A 3s spawn grace prevents immediate attacks. While alive anywhere on the map, `isMovementReversed` reverses raw queued input once when choosing the next player tile in `updatePlayer`, so keyboard, swipe, and joystick share the same behavior. Existing in-flight steps finish normally; death/debug replacement immediately restores the mapping for subsequent steps. Do not invert the stored queue or page-level inputs again.

`prayerFleePath` uses floor BFS distances from the player and from the boss to choose a reachable escape tile, with a small travel-distance penalty; recalculates every 30 ticks and can route across hallways. `BOSS_NAMES`, `BOSS_TYPES`, and `isBossType` now define the shared seven-boss roster used for natural/debug spawns, exclusivity, meteor treatment, reward/cooldown handling, ally exclusions, and minimap recognition. Normal 75%-max-HP meteor damage applies. Killing Blind Prayer grants the existing boss restoration and starts the 42-second death-to-next-spawn delay. Its curse is derived from live enemies, so no separate debuff state needs cleanup.

Blind Prayer rituals begin every 30s while the Ruby is placed, with a 1.5s warning around the source and destination. Picking up or moving the Ruby cancels the cast and resets the cooldown. Destination is another chamber center (`TELEPORT_PADS`), excluding active meteor warnings at both selection and resolution. No direct Ruby damage. `holy1.mp3` (ritual) and `holy2.mp3` (escape attack) both play at full 1.0 volume, intentionally independent of the shared attack trim. Ability cooldowns and FX pause in the teleport menu. White glyphs are shared by the body, ritual, and six-tile attack ring; `prayerWaves` decays in `tick`.

God Mode `No Mobs` toggles `godSetNoMobs`: clears regular enemies without kill rewards and disables their spawn timer; preserves bosses and Queen echoes. It is independent of immortality, persists across PLAY AGAIN, and resets on a fresh load.

Prayer knockback uses `playerKnockback`, finishes an in-flight step before moving one cardinal tile at 8 px/tick, preserves raw held input, and is cleared by teleport. Damage-absorption shields do not block the push.

## Fiery King: neglect punishment

`KingAbilities` tracks `untouchedTicks` and `arenaWindupTicks` independently of the speed ramp (`phaseTimer`). After 600 ticks (10s) without actual HP damage, a 390-tick (6.5s) warning warms the whole viewport and displays “KING’S WRATH — HIT THE KING!”. Any HP damage through `damageEnemy` (including meteors and chain damage) resets both counters, interrupting the warning. If ignored, the pulse deals 15–30 damage to the player and placed Ruby (linear whole-point growth with this King’s age, capped at 30 after 350s, alongside max speed) anywhere, including other chambers and hallways; existing damage shields and carried-core safety apply normally. It restarts the full timer after firing. God-mode immunity and teleport-menu pause still apply. Warning state belongs to the live King, so death/debug replacement clears it automatically; the brief impact flash uses `kingArenaPulseTicks`. Normal/debug spawns initialize fresh counters.

King uses the generated soft `kingFireWarning.wav` warning and the user-supplied `kingFireSpell.mp3` impact (original filename: dragon-studio-fire-spell-impact-393921.mp3). No additional explosion clip is layered. The warning starts 5s earlier while total untouched-to-pulse remains 16.5s.

Frost Warden shield meteor immunity: `updateMeteorite` skips any enemy with `icyShieldHP > 0` before its normal/boss damage dispatch. The meteor consumes neither shield HP nor enemy HP. A shield broken by player attacks no longer grants this protection. Ordinary Shielder damage reduction does not grant meteor immunity; Warden herself retains normal boss meteor damage unless carrying an actual ice shield.

King speed and arena damage share `KING_RAMP_SECONDS` (350). Only a new King resets age; hits interrupt/reset the neglect countdown without resetting speed or damage growth.

Enemies with an active Frost Warden shield at player-bomb detonation take at most their remaining shield HP from that entire blast, with no HP overflow. Bomb-triggered chain lightning uses the same protection snapshot so hit order cannot bypass it after the shield breaks. Subsequent attacks/bombs deal normal damage once the shield is gone. Other player attacks retain normal damage.

Full-energy player visuals: `drawFullEnergyAura` shows a gold eight-stream pixel starburst plus an overhead eight-point star whenever starEnergy is full or poweredTicks > 0. Active power speeds up/extends the rays. This is separate from the Sniper buff square/electric aura, and both can coexist. No text or gameplay changes.

Charge audio now uses the user-supplied `public/sounds/charging.wav`. `startChargeSound` plays one shared audio instance once per hold, stretched to fill the charge window (~0.74x normally, ~0.45x while chilled); `stopChargeSound` cancels/resets it on release, wave firing, cooldown, teleport/loss, rules/unmount, window blur, visibility loss, or restart. A holding latch prevents replay if the sample ends just before firing. Tap charges use the same sample without looping. Avoid replaying/stacking the 0.59s sample at the old 120ms synth cadence.

Blind Prayer cloth is 86% opaque black, with three closed stepped eyelids beneath it. No visible pupils.

## Sea Chanter (`sea_chanter`)

Dark-ocean-blue finned siren alien, with violet-blue fins and the original two-eyed siren face, gills, pearl scales and small singing mouth. Both pearl eyes retain their original equal 11×6 size. All recent facial redesigns, including the enlarged left eye, were reverted. Underwater back sprite stays unchanged. Base 100 HP, surface speed 3.8. Hunting mode chases the player and fires a ranged song: traveling wave deals 12 player damage, splash 17–7 by distance, no Ruby damage or melee, 12 tiles, 24-tick locked-position telegraph, traveling at 20 px/tick, ignoring walls for targeting and travel. Cooldown 210 ticks after release, first shot delay 120 ticks. Pulsing stepped water-ripple target marker (no aiming line), pixel music notes, rolling layered blue water sheet with a curling stepped white foam crest, dense square droplets, and expanding water columns/foam on impact. Wave travels to the locked aim point and splashes within 6 tiles (12-tile diameter), damage falls linearly with distance from 17 at center to 7 at edge, rounded to nearest integer; a player hit by the traveling wave cannot also take splash damage from that cast. Waves pass through walls and splash damage ignores cover. Splash visuals fade over 24 ticks; User audio: seaSinging.mp3 plays its complete 0.74s note at natural 1x speed; launch after 0.4s overlaps the remaining note and does not cut it off (nor does diving), seaSong.mp3 on wave release, seaDive.mp3 on dive entry, seaHeal.mp3 on recovery/support. Measured source RMS: dive -8.8 dBFS, attack -10.2, healing -22.1, singing -18.3; gains respectively .28/.30/.52/.58 multiplied by ATTACK_SFX_VOLUME. Healing uses only a 1.6s fading excerpt, throttled to once per 180 ticks; Diving layers only seaSinging4.mp3 (10.06s, gain .32 times attack mix) under the transition sound; Singing 2 and 3 are unused; long singing stops on resurfacing. Cue voices cannot stack with themselves and stop on death/replacement/restart.

Sea Chanter has no global healing debuff. Each surfaced periodic support pulse applies 30% reduced player healing for 180 ticks (3s) when within its 12-tile radius, even with no wounded allies. On-hit ally healing does not apply/refresh the debuff. Pulses refresh duration without stacking; leaving range lets it expire, diving stops new pulses, and death/debug replacement/restart clears it. All player healing sources are affected while active, rounded to one decimal; Ruby healing and shields are unchanged. A pulsing blue crossed healing icon appears beside the character with no text. Dive entry plays water/singing audio but no healing cue; healing crosses and healing audio only trigger on actual conditional recovery.

At <=40% HP she dives at 4.2 speed, wandering on random 4–10-tile routes when the player is farther than 18 tiles (roughly one chamber width). Within 18 tiles she switches to escape routing; once safely outside it, she drops the escape route and resumes roaming. Roam destinations avoid dead-end corners and reselect when reached or unreachable. She takes 50% extra HP damage from all sources while diving (after shield absorption; bullet immunity and ice-shield meteor/bomb rules still apply). She has no passive self-regeneration; at >=75% she resurfaces. A 36-tick cosmetic emergence splash uses two expanding stepped ripples and 28 blue water jets with pearl foam, anchored at her emergence position; the effect pauses with the game and does no damage. Hysteresis prevents mode flicker. While surfaced she automatically heals normal allies within 12 tiles by 3 HP every 180 ticks (3 seconds). The pulse pauses while diving; resurfacing starts a fresh 3-second countdown. Passive and on-hit support share the same range, max-HP clamp, recipient flash, caster ripple and throttled healing sound. Player HP damage to her additionally heals living normal allies within a 12-tile circular radius by 10 HP per hit, including across chamber/hallway boundaries (clamped to max). While diving, player HP damage to normal aliens anywhere heals her by 80% of actual HP lost, capped at 25 per hit/alien. Shields, overkill, meteors, enemy damage, bosses and Queen echoes cannot feed this healing. Dead casters never regenerate. Underwater sprite is a faint (~34% opacity), narrower back/dorsal-fin view with denser bubbles, pixel healing crosses and a stepped wake; no face. HP bar remains visible underwater at 34% opacity, matching the body.

`isSeaChanterDiving` is the shared targeting/render predicate. Both minimap dots and boss icons use isEnemyVisibleOnMinimap: surfaced Sea Chanter is always visible; while diving she is hidden for 7 seconds then revealed for 1 second, repeating every 8 seconds. The per-dive clock resets on dive entry and pauses with gameplay. Bullet aim selection and existing projectile collisions skip her; laser, wave, bomb, lightning, and meteor damage still work. Standard seven-boss exclusivity, rewards, meteor rules, 42s death-to-next-spawn timing, and debug spawn apply. No Mobs preserves her. Abilities pause in the teleport menu.

Sea Chanter palette is ocean blue (#3998d6) including her entire minimap marker; Storm Reaper is lighter mint-cyan (#52ffd6). Room labels are now clockwise ALPHA(TL), BETA(TR), GAMMA(BR), DELTA(BL); geometry stays indexed TL=0, TR=1, BL=2, BR=3 and meteor sequence remains [0,1,3,2].


Soul Devourer long-range damage balance: base player damage 10 and Ruby damage 8, quadratic stack multiplier 1 + (stacks/20)^2 (2x at full stacks). At 0/5/10/15/20 stacks: player 10/11/13/16/20, Ruby 8/9/10/13/16, rounded to integer. This replaces the former 7.5x cap (90 player / 105 Ruby). Attack cooldown remains 55 ticks.

Blind Prayer spawn warning: while bossSpawnTicks is active (4 seconds) and a live Prayer is reversing movement, show a yellow MOVEMENT REVERSED banner below the spawn announcement, styled like the King pulse warning. Uses the shared paused announcement timer and disappears immediately if Prayer dies or is replaced.


## Thorn Weaver (thorn_weaver)

Eighth boss: male plant alien with a fully square bark body scaled to match Shadow Queen (bodyFraction 0.95, 45px body at TILE_SIZE 48; all decorations scale with it), a single fully rectangular brown inset with subtle brown grain and a simple flat pale rectangular central eye with a thick 9×13 dark pupil and no highlight/glow, plus six small rectangular eyes (three above and three below). Crooked leafy hood, one off-centre pink flower on a bent stalk, thin open vine tendrils and three splayed root feet.
